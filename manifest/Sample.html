Ambassador Guidelines

Welcome to Launched Connect

Launched Connect helps students connect with Student Ambassadors to learn more about student life, university experiences, courses, accommodation, activities and more.

As a Student Ambassador, you play an important role in helping students feel welcomed, informed and confident about university life.

Please follow these guidelines whenever you use Launched Connect.

1. Be respectful and welcoming

• Treat every student with respect and kindness.
• Be polite, patient and welcoming.
• Respect different backgrounds, cultures, opinions and experiences.
• Do not use offensive, threatening or discriminatory language.
• Do not bully, harass or intimidate students.

2. Keep conversations appropriate

Use Launched Connect for appropriate student-related conversations.

You can talk about:

• Student life
• Courses and studying
• Accommodation and halls
• Clubs and societies
• University facilities
• Campus life
• The local area
• Hobbies and interests
• Your own student experience
• Preparing for university

Keep conversations relevant to the purpose of Launched Connect.

3. Protect personal information

Do not ask students for sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Never request or share:

• Passwords
• Bank or card details
• Financial information
• Identity documents
• Security information
• Other sensitive personal information

Keep conversations within Launched Connect wherever possible.

4. Stay within your role

Student Ambassadors share their own experiences and provide peer support. You are not responsible for making official university decisions.

Do not guarantee:

• Admission
• Scholarships
• Accommodation
• Employment
• Course outcomes
• Other university decisions

If a student needs official information or support, direct them to the appropriate university team.

5. Safeguarding and student wellbeing

If a student shares something that causes concern about their safety, wellbeing or safeguarding, do not attempt to manage the situation yourself.

• Listen respectfully.
• Do not promise confidentiality.
• Do not investigate the situation yourself.
• Report concerns through the appropriate university process.
• Contact the appropriate support team when required.

6. Keep messages appropriate

Please keep your messages:

• Friendly
• Clear
• Relevant
• Respectful
• Professional

Do not send:

• Sexual or inappropriate content
• Offensive images or messages
• Threats
• Harassment
• Discriminatory comments
• Abusive messages
• Unwanted or inappropriate contact requests

7. Respect student boundaries

• Do not pressure a student to continue a conversation.
• Respect their decision if they do not want to discuss a topic.
• Do not request unnecessary personal contact details.
• Keep communication within Launched Connect where possible.
• Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.

8. Keep your profile accurate

Your Ambassador profile should accurately reflect your current student experience and interests.

• Keep your profile information up to date.
• Make sure your interests and languages are accurate.
• Update your availability when necessary.
• Do not include inappropriate or misleading information.

9. Availability and response times

Students may contact you at different times. You are not expected to respond immediately.

• Keep your availability status accurate.
• Respond when you are reasonably available.
• Do not feel pressured to respond outside your availability.
• Let the appropriate team know if you are unable to continue as an Ambassador.

10. Report a concern

Report a conversation if you experience:

• Bullying or harassment
• Threatening behaviour
• Discrimination
• Sexual or inappropriate messages
• Requests for sensitive personal information
• Safeguarding concerns
• Any behaviour that makes you feel unsafe or uncomfortable

Use the available Report or moderation functionality where provided, and contact the appropriate university team when necessary.

11. Looking after yourself

Supporting students can be rewarding, but it is important to look after your own wellbeing too.

• Take breaks when needed.
• Do not feel responsible for solving every student concern.
• Ask the university team for support when needed.
• Maintain appropriate boundaries between your Ambassador role and personal life.

12. Remember

Be respectful. Be safe. Be supportive. Be professional.

Your role as a Student Ambassador is to share your experience, support students and help them feel more confident about university life.

If you need support or have a concern about a student interaction, please contact the appropriate university team.

Contact the Launched Connect Team

If you have a question, need support, or want to report a concern, please contact the Launched Connect team through the support channel provided by your university.

By using Launched Connect as a Student Ambassador, you agree to follow these guidelines and the university's relevant policies and procedures.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read and agree to the Launched Connect Ambassador Guidelines.
